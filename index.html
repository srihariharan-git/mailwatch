What it does

MailWatch watches your email inbox — Gmail, Office 365, or any college account — and the moment a sender you care about emails you, it pops a Windows desktop notification with an AI-written summary of the email.

  • Instant toasts — no browser tab needs to stay open
  • Gemini AI summarizes each email in one line
  • You choose exactly who triggers alerts: boss@company.com or @company.com
  • Runs quietly in the background, auto-starts on login
  • Your credentials never leave your own PC

Set up in one minute

  1. Download the ZIP and extract it. (No GitHub account needed.)
  2. Need Node.js (v18+) — install if you don't have it.
  3. In the folder, run npm install then npm start.
  4. Open http://localhost:3000 — a setup form greets you. Enter your email, your Gmail app password (create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords), and an optional Gemini API key (aistudio.google.com/apikey).
  5. Click Test & save — done. MailWatch validates your login, installs itself to auto-start, and begins watching.

Free and open-source. No cloud account, no subscription, no data collected.

Why run it yourself?

Unlike email apps that upload your whole mailbox to a server, MailWatch runs entirely on your machine. Your password stays in a local .env file, and the only thing sent anywhere is the text of one email — to Google's Gemini — when you've opted in to summaries.
